## 1. Regimes and structural breaks

Panel 1960Q1 to 2023Q2 (254 quarters).

**Hamilton (1989) Markov-switching model of real GDP growth** (switching mean, impulse dummies for 2020Q2-Q3):

| | recession regime | expansion regime |
|---|---|---|
| mean growth, % ann. | -2.16 | 3.59 |
| expected duration, quarters | 2.8 | 24.3 |

Agreement with NBER recession dates (AUC): **0.981** with full-sample parameters, **0.987** in real time over 2000Q1 to 2023Q2 (parameters re-estimated with data available at each quarter only).

**Variance breaks in GDP growth** (Bai-Perron dynamic programming, BIC): 1980Q2, 1984Q3; growth volatility by segment 3.91, 5.52, 2.33 pp (the Great Moderation). **Variance breaks in earnings growth**: 2007Q3, 2011Q3 (the 2008-09 write-down era).

## 4. Live forecast and explanations

Production model re-estimated on the full sample; 4-quarter log growth (%) from the last observation, with 90% intervals from the errors realised to date. For earnings, after the large 2020-2022 misses, ACI has pushed its working quantile level above 1: no finite interval built from the last 40 quarters of errors would have delivered 90% coverage, so ACI reports the interval as unbounded and the drift check in section 6 flags the same model.
